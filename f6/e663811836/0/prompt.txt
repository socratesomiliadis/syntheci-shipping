We need to rework our system to have the following embeddings: (Also some of this stuff may be missing from our demo-data so we need to make additions based on our current vessels, storylines etc)

What should become embeddings?
1. Emails

Embed emails, but not as one huge blob.

For each email, create chunks like:

Subject
Sender / recipients
Date
Cleaned body
Important quoted context, if needed
Linked attachments
Detected vessel / voyage / job / port

Example embedding text:

Email subject: MV ATHENA / Piraeus / PDA Rev 2
From: Piraeus Agent
To: Operations Greece
Date: 2026-04-27
Body:
Please find attached PDA Revision 2 for MV ATHENA. Berth prospects have changed and ETA is now 19:30 local time...
Detected entities:
Vessel: MV ATHENA
Port: Piraeus
Document: PDA Revision 2
Voyage: VOY-2026-018

Do not embed raw email HTML, signatures, disclaimers, huge quoted chains, tracking pixels, or repeated footer text.

2. Email threads

Also create a thread-level summary embedding.

This is very useful because users often ask:

What is the latest status of this voyage?
What changed in this thread?
What are the open items?

For each thread, store:

Thread summary
Timeline
Latest status
Open questions
Decisions
Missing documents
Linked attachments

So you have both:

individual email embeddings
thread summary embeddings

This gives better retrieval than only embedding every email separately.

3. Attachments

Yes, definitely embed attachments.

Especially:

PDFs;
DOCX files;
charter parties;
recaps;
SOFs;
NORs;
PDAs;
FDAs;
invoices;
port documents;
inspection reports;
bunker docs;
claim documents.

For long documents, chunk by:

page
section
clause
heading
table

For a charter party, do not just chunk every 1,000 tokens blindly. Try to preserve clauses.

Example:

Document: CharterParty_MV_Athena.pdf
Page: 7
Section: Laytime and Demurrage
Text:
Laytime shall be 72 hours SHINC...
4. Contacts

Usually do not embed full contacts as primary content.

Instead, store contacts as structured data:

name
email
company
role
phone
associated vessels/voyages
counterparty type

But you can embed a small contact profile if useful:

Contact: John Smith
Company: Piraeus Port Agency
Role: Port agent
Known for: Handles Piraeus port calls
Related voyages: VOY-2026-018, VOY-2026-021

This helps with questions like:

Who is the agent for Piraeus?
Have we worked with this counterparty before?
5. Vessels, voyages, jobs

Yes, but as entity profiles, not raw database rows.

Create an embedding for each vessel/voyage/job summary:

Voyage: VOY-2026-018
Vessel: MV ATHENA
Load port: Alexandria
Discharge port: Piraeus
Cargo: wheat
Current status: awaiting berth
Open issues: final SOF missing, PDA Rev 2 pending finance review
Related documents: charter party, PDA Rev 2, draft SOF

This is one of the most important things for maritime RAG.

The user does not always ask for a document. They ask about an operational object:

What is going on with MV Athena?
What are the risks for this voyage?
What changed since yesterday?

A voyage summary embedding helps answer that.

6. Structured operational records

Embed summaries of records from systems like IMOS, Dataloy, or custom databases.

For example:

Port call record
Voyage record
Bunker record
Claim record
Invoice record
Inspection record

Do not embed every field blindly. Convert important fields into readable text.

Example:

Port call record:
Vessel MV ATHENA arrived Piraeus on 2026-04-27. Agent is Piraeus Port Agency. Current status is awaiting berth. PDA Revision 2 received. FDA pending.

Store the raw fields separately as metadata.
